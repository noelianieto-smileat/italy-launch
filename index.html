The big picture

How the month went

Italy is a new market for Smileat. In one month we have started building a community and a list of interested families, right before opening the online store.

1

1,625 families have already signed up

The landing page already has a list of people waiting for the launch. Meta ads have brought 855 of those sign-ups, at €1.65 each.

2

We are already 1,700 on Instagram

The followers campaign has generated 24,723 profile visits, at just 3.5 cents each, and 497 new followers from the ads.

3

They find us on Google

457 people clicked on Google when searching for Smileat, with a 3.3% CTR (the share of times they saw us and clicked in).

How to read the data: Meta covers 14 September to 19 October and Google 1 September to 15 October. The 1,625 sign-ups and 1,700 followers are today's overall totals; everything else is what the ad platforms measure.

What we have learned

What works in Italy

Showing the product works

The video of how the jars are filled ("Cosa c'è davvero qui dentro?") was the content with the most profile visits, at a very low cost.

Useful content gets followed

The feeding guides (Feeding and Legumes) won more followers than the video, with far less investment: parents want to learn.

October is off to a good start

The strongest October creative (AD4 OCT) gets each sign-up for €1.54, versus €1.73 for the best September one (AD1).

We test and scale

We will keep launching new creatives, measuring what works and putting more budget behind the winners.
